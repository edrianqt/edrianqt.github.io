AlagaMind

A mental wellness platform that pairs an AI companion with private journaling and guided CBT exercises. It speaks the languages people in the Visayas and Mindanao actually use, so support never needs translating first.

What it does

  • AI companionSupportive conversations any time of day, in your own language.
  • Smart journalPrivate entries, stored securely and easy to organize.
  • CBT exercisesStep-by-step sessions at beginner to advanced levels.
  • Cognitive reframingGuided practice for turning around negative thoughts.
  • Mood trackingA quick daily check-in that builds a history over time.
  • Sentiment analysisDetects the emotion in journal entries and tracks it.
  • Pattern insightsCharts that show emotional trends across weeks.
  • Crisis resourcesHotline links one tap away from any conversation.

My part

  • Built the responsive interfaces for the conversational flows in Cebuano, Tagalog and English.
  • Built the daily mood tracking screens and the crisis-resource links.
  • Ran functional and usability testing across every workflow, in all three languages.
