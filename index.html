AlagaMind

A mental wellness platform that pairs an AI companion with private journaling and guided CBT exercises. It speaks Cebuano, Tagalog and English, so people in the Visayas and Mindanao don't have to translate how they feel first.

What it does

  • AI companionSupportive conversations any time, in your own language.
  • Smart journalPrivate entries, stored securely and easy to organize.
  • CBT exercisesStep-by-step sessions from beginner to advanced.
  • Cognitive reframingGuided practice for turning around negative thoughts.
  • Mood trackingA quick daily check-in that builds a history.
  • Sentiment analysisDetects the emotion in journal entries over time.
  • Pattern insightsCharts that show emotional trends across weeks.
  • Crisis resourcesHotline links one tap away from any conversation.
